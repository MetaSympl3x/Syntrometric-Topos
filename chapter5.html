In an \((\infty,1)\)-topos, composition is governed by coherent higher homotopies rather than brittle strict equality.

Definition 5.7 & Proposition 5.8: Syntroclinic Bridge Adjunction For grades \(n \ge 0\) and \(N \ge 1\), the Syntroclinic Bridge is the left adjoint extension-of-scalars functor: \[ B_{n, n+N} \;\coloneqq\; (\phi_{n, n+N})_! \colon \Alg_{\On}(\Xsyn) \;\rightleftarrows\; \Alg_{\mathcal{O}_{n+N}}(\Xsyn) \;\colon (\phi_{n, n+N})^* =: R_{n+N, n} \] On the subcategory \(\mathcal{I}_n \subseteq \Met_n(\Xsyn)\) of invariant syndromes, the restricted bridge \(B_{n, n+N}|_{\mathcal{I}_n}\) is fully faithful if and only if for every \(X \in \operatorname{Obj}(\mathcal{I}_n)\), the adjunction unit is an equivalence in \(\Met_n(\Xsyn)\): \[ \eta_X \colon X \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; R_{n+N, n}\big(B_{n, n+N}(X)\big) \]

Proof of Proposition 5.8 (Standard Adjunction Criterion for Fully Faithful Lifting): For any \(X, Y \in \operatorname{Obj}(\mathcal{I}_n)\), the adjunction \(B_{n, n+N} \dashv R_{n+N, n}\) provides a natural homotopy equivalence of mapping spaces: \[ \Map_{\Alg_{\mathcal{O}_{n+N}}}\!\big(B_{n, n+N}(X),\, B_{n, n+N}(Y)\big) \;\simeq\; \Map_{\Alg_{\On}}\!\big(X,\, R_{n+N, n}(B_{n, n+N}(Y))\big) \] Under this equivalence, the action of \(B_{n, n+N}\) on mapping spaces \(\Map(X, Y) \to \Map(B(X), B(Y))\) factors as post-composition with the unit morphism \((\eta_Y)_* \colon \Map(X, Y) \to \Map(X, R(B(Y)))\).
