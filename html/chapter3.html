Historical Vignette: The Categorical Turn in Physics

Introduced by Samuel Eilenberg and Saunders Mac Lane in 1945 to formalize algebraic topology, category theory shifted mathematics from the internal elements of sets to the structural morphisms between objects. In the late 20th and early 21st centuries, John Baez, James Dolan, Samson Abramsky, and Bob Coecke pioneered categorical quantum mechanics and compositional network theory, modeling physical couplings via symmetric monoidal categories, spans, and pushouts. Reconstructing Heim’s Syntrixkorporationen as universal colimits over decorated interaction trees situates his 1982 network calculus directly within modern compositional physics.

Intuition: Combining Two Instruments — Parallel Wiring vs. Shared Channels

When two laboratory instruments are placed side by side without a cable between them, their readout channels simply add (\(p+q\), a coproduct \(C_m\)). When they are phase-locked across \(\lambda\) shared calibration channels, the shared wires are identified rather than counted twice, leaving \(p+q-\lambda\) independent channels (a span pushout \(K_m\)).

Theorem 3.4: Exact Pushout Sequence & Dimension Subtraction Formula

Let \(\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}} \coloneqq \operatorname{rank}(\delta_\phi) = \dim R_\phi - \dim(\ker \phi_a \cap \ker \phi_b)\). When \(\phi_a, \phi_b\) are jointly injective (\(\ker \phi_a \cap \ker \phi_b = \{0\}\)), \(\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}} = \lambda\) and \(\dim(P_a \boxplus_\phi P_b) = \mathbf{p + q - \lambda}\).

Proof of Theorem 3.4 (Rank-Nullity & Quotient Norm Completeness)
  1. Step 1 (Closed Subspace): Since \(P_a \oplus_1 P_b\) is finite-dimensional, \(\operatorname{Im}(\delta_\phi)\) is automatically closed, so the quotient seminorm \(\|\cdot\|_{\boxplus}\) is a complete norm and \(\pi_{\boxplus}\) has operator norm \(\le 1\).
